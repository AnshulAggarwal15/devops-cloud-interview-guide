## Question  
What is your contribution in the team? (for candidates with > 5 years of experience)

### 📝 Short Explanation  
For a **senior** candidate, the interviewer expects contributions beyond individual tasks — **ownership, mentoring, technical decisions, and driving improvements** across the team. Show leadership and impact, not just execution.

## ✅ Answer  
As a senior engineer, my contribution goes beyond delivering my own tasks: I **own critical systems**, **drive architecture and tooling decisions**, **mentor junior engineers**, set standards (IaC, CI/CD, reviews), and lead incident response and postmortems. I focus on making the **whole team** more effective and the platform more reliable.

### 📘 Detailed Explanation  

#### 🏗️ 1. Ownership & technical leadership
- Own critical infrastructure/services end to end.
- Drive **design decisions** (e.g. choosing GitOps, standardizing Terraform modules).
- Balance trade-offs (cost, reliability, delivery speed) and justify them.

#### 👨‍🏫 2. Mentoring & enablement
- Mentor junior/mid engineers, review their work, and unblock them.
- Create **runbooks, standards, and documentation** so the team scales.

#### 🔧 3. Process & quality improvements
- Improve CI/CD reliability and speed, enforce **code review and IaC standards**.
- Introduce practices like **SLOs**, better alerting, security guardrails.

#### 🚨 4. Incident leadership
- Lead **incident response**, coordinate during outages, run **blameless postmortems**, and drive preventive actions.

#### 🤝 5. Cross-team influence
- Partner with dev, security, and product; represent DevOps in planning.

---

### 🧠 Real-world Example

> “Beyond my own delivery, I owned our EKS platform and led the move to **GitOps with Argo CD**, which standardized how every team deployed. I mentored two junior engineers, set our Terraform module standards, and led incident response — including postmortems that cut repeat incidents. My goal is always to raise the whole team's baseline, not just ship my own tickets.”

> Summary:  
> As a senior, frame contribution as **ownership + technical decisions + mentoring + process/quality + incident leadership + cross-team influence**. Emphasize how you multiply the **team's** effectiveness, backed by a concrete example.
