## Question  
Tell us about your DevOps experience.

### 📝 Short Explanation  
A broad "introduce yourself professionally" question. The interviewer wants a **structured summary** of your background, the tools/domains you know, and the impact you've had — not your life story. Keep it tight and outcome-focused.

## ✅ Answer  
I give a short, structured summary: my years of experience and role, the **core areas** I work in (CI/CD, containers/Kubernetes, cloud, IaC, observability), the **tools** I'm strongest with, and **one or two concrete outcomes** I've delivered — then invite them to go deeper anywhere.

### 📘 Detailed Explanation  

Use a simple structure so it lands in under a minute:

#### 🧭 1. Headline
- "I'm a DevOps/Cloud engineer with X years of experience, currently working on \[platform/product]."

#### 🧰 2. Core areas + tools
| Area | Tools |
|---|---|
| CI/CD | Jenkins, GitHub Actions, Argo CD |
| Containers/Orchestration | Docker, Kubernetes (EKS) |
| Cloud | AWS (VPC, EC2, IAM, RDS, S3) |
| IaC | Terraform |
| Observability | Prometheus, Grafana, Loki |
| Scripting | Bash, Python |

#### 📈 3. Impact (the part they remember)
- Quantify: "cut deployment time from X to Y", "reduced cloud cost by ~30%", "improved MTTR by adding tracing".

#### 🔚 4. Hand-off
- "Happy to go deeper into any of these" — invites the conversation forward.

---

### 🧠 Real-world Example

> “I'm a DevOps engineer with around 4 years' experience. I mainly work on **CI/CD (Jenkins, Argo CD)**, **Kubernetes on EKS**, and **AWS infrastructure managed with Terraform**, plus observability with Prometheus and Grafana. Recently I built a GitOps deployment flow that cut release time significantly and set up SLO-based alerting that reduced our false pages. I'm happy to dive into any of those.”

> Summary:  
> Answer with **headline → core areas & tools → quantified impact → invite follow-up**. Keep it under a minute and focus on **outcomes**, letting the interviewer pull the threads they care about.
