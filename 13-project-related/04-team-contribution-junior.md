## Question  
What is your contribution in the team? (for candidates with < 5 years of experience)

### 📝 Short Explanation  
For a candidate earlier in their career, the interviewer wants to see **reliable delivery, initiative, willingness to learn, and good collaboration** — solid execution and ownership of your tasks, not necessarily team-wide leadership.

## ✅ Answer  
I contribute by **reliably delivering my assigned work** (pipelines, IaC changes, deployments, automation), taking **ownership** of tasks end to end, actively **learning** the stack, helping teammates where I can, and improving small things I notice — like flaky pipelines or missing documentation.

### 📘 Detailed Explanation  

#### ✅ 1. Reliable execution
- Deliver tasks on time with quality: CI/CD changes, Terraform updates, Kubernetes deployments, scripts.
- Test my changes and follow team standards.

#### 🙋 2. Ownership & initiative
- Take a task from start to finish, ask good questions, and follow through.
- Proactively fix small pain points (a flaky test, a slow build step, an outdated doc).

#### 📚 3. Continuous learning
- Ramp up on the team's tools and the "why" behind decisions.
- Apply feedback from code reviews quickly.

#### 🤝 4. Collaboration
- Participate in standups, do reviews at my level, and share what I learn.
- Escalate blockers early instead of getting stuck silently.

#### 🔧 5. Automation mindset
- Look for repetitive manual work to script/automate.

---

### 🧠 Real-world Example

> “In my current role I own our CI/CD pipeline changes and day-to-day Kubernetes deployments. I noticed our builds were slow, so I added caching and cut build time noticeably. I pick up tickets end to end, ask when I'm unsure, apply review feedback, and document what I learn so the next person has it easier. I'm focused on being dependable and leveling up fast.”

> Summary:  
> For under 5 years, frame it as **reliable delivery + ownership/initiative + fast learning + collaboration + automation mindset**. Show you're dependable and growing, with a concrete example of a small improvement you drove.
